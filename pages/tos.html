Terms of Service & Code of Conduct

Last Updated: October 2026 • Version 2.0

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and utilizing Students Hub, you agree to be bound by the terms and provisions of this agreement. In addition, when using specific services such as event registration and forums, you agree to comply with posted academic guidelines.

2. Academic Integrity

Students Hub is designed to foster genuine collaboration and peer learning. Users are strictly prohibited from using the platform to share live examination answers, plagiarize code for graded laboratory assignments, or violate institutional academic regulations.

3. User Conduct and Content

You agree to use the forum, event boards, and study groups solely for lawful, educational objectives. Harassment, abusive language, spamming, and the distribution of malicious scripts are strictly forbidden and will result in permanent account suspension.

4. Privacy, Password Security and Data Usage

We respect your personal privacy. Student credentials and passwords are encrypted and salted using PHP's standard password_hash() with bcrypt. Plaintext passwords are never logged or stored. Personal academic records are not shared with third-party advertisers.

5. Modification of Service

The student development team reserves the right to enhance or alter features on the platform with appropriate notice. Feedback may be submitted directly through our dedicated Feedback Portal.

6. Contact and Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding these Terms or wish to appeal an account decision, please contact the campus administrator via our Contact Us page.
