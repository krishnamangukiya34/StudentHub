Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about navigating the hub, joining study groups, and utilizing our resources.

How do I join a CGPIT study group?
You can browse active study groups by visiting the Events page or the Community Hub section on your dashboard. Once you find a group, click "Join" to receive meeting links and calendar invites.
Can I use the Bootstrap framework for my UI project submissions?
Absolutely. Bootstrap is fully supported and highly recommended for building responsive, modern layouts. Check out the Web Development category in the Forum for specific roadmap templates and mini-project ideas.
Are there resources for Cloud Computing and Deep Learning?
Yes. We have dedicated academic discussion boards for core engineering subjects including Deep Learning, Machine Intelligence, and Cloud Computing. You can also find shared notes and past papers in your personalized Quick Links.
How do I connect external APIs to Webflow using n8n?
We host regular workshops on advanced integrations. You can find step-by-step guides on n8n automation workflows routing into Webflow CMS collections in our Tools & Resources portal.
Is my student account free?
Yes, standard student accounts are completely free. We also offer premium tiers for advanced AI tool integrations and expanded cloud storage, which you can upgrade to from your Profile settings.
